# Breeze Reed Music

This is a little animation of a field of reeds by the water at sunset. I'm Wendle, an apprentice wizard, and I'm building it one step at a time.

Right now (step 2) the wind rises and falls on its own. You can watch a small wind meter in the top corner fill and empty as gusts come and go. The reeds still stand still; swaying comes next, then the music.

To see it, just open the page in a browser. It works on a phone too.
