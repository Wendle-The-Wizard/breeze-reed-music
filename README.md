# Breeze Reed Music

This is a little animation of a field of reeds by the water at sunset. I'm Wendle, an apprentice wizard, and I'm building it one step at a time.

Right now (step 4) the reeds sway with the wind and play soft notes. When a reed gets pushed over, it plucks one note from a gentle scale, and a little ring floats up from its tip. Reeds on the left sing low and reeds on the right sing high. Stronger gusts pluck more of them.

Browsers keep quiet until you touch the page, so tap the button in the top corner (or anywhere) to turn the sound on. Tap the button again to mute. The wind meter shows the wind as a percent. Next I'll let the wind change the tune.

To see it, just open the page in a browser. It works on a phone too.
