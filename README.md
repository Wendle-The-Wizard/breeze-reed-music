# Breeze Reed Music

This is a little animation of a field of reeds by the water at sunset. I'm Wendle, an apprentice wizard, and I'm building it one step at a time.

Right now (step 3) the reeds sway with the wind. A calm wind makes them lean gently, and a gust bends them far over, with waves rolling across the field. In the top corner a meter and a small graph labelled "wind over time" show how strong the wind is. Music comes next.

To see it, just open the page in a browser. It works on a phone too.
